Chapter XIII: Punishment for Violating Justice

B04.C13 · Book IV: The Removal of Thorns · p. 331–337

Punishment for Violating Justice

P001HE who causes a Bráhman to partake of whatever food or drink is prohibited shall be punished with the highest amercement. He who causes a Kshatriya to do the same shall be punished with the middlemost amercement; a Vaisya, with the first amercement; and a Súdra, with a fine of 54 panas.p.33154 panasfine 54 panas54bráhmansúdrabrahmankshatriyasudravaisyaamercementfinefirst-amercementhighest-amercementmiddlemost-amercementpunishment

P002Those who voluntarily partake of whatever is condemned either as food or drink shall be outcast.p.332

P003He who forces his entrance into another's house during the day shall be punished with the first amercement; and during the night with the middlemost. Any person who with weapon in hand enters into another's house either during the day or night shall be punished with the highest amercement.p.332amercementfirst-amercementhighest-amercementpunishment

P004When beggars or peddlers and lunatics or mad persons attempt to enter into a house by force, or when neighbours force their entrance into a house in danger, they shall not be punished provided no such entrance is specially prohibited.p.332punishment

P005He who mounts the roof of his own house after midnight shall be punished with the first amercement; and of another's house, with the middlemost amercement.p.332amercementfirst-amercementmiddlemost-amercementpunishment

P006Those who break the fences of villages, gardens, or fields shall also be punished with the middlemost amercement.p.332amercementmiddlemost-amercementpunishment

P007Having made the value, etc., of their merchandise known (to the headman of the village), traders shall halt in some part of a village. When any part of their merchandise which has not been truly sent out of the village during the night has been stolen or lost, the headman of the village shall make good the loss.p.332merchant

P008Whatever of their merchandise is stolen or lost in the intervening places between any two villages shall the superintendent of pasture lands make good. If there are no pasture lands (in such places), the officer called Chorarajjuka shall make good the loss. If the loss of merchandise occurs in such parts of the country as are not provided even with such security (a Chorarajjuka), the people in the boundaries of the place shall contribute to make up the loss. If there are no people in the boundaries, the people of five or ten villages of the neighbourhood shall make up the loss.p.332fivetentwoofficer

P009Harm due to the construction of unstable houses, carts with no support or with a beam or weapon hung above or with damaged support or with no covering, and harm due to causing a cart to fall in pits, or a tank, or from a dam, shall be treated as assault.p.333

P010Cutting of trees, stealing the rope with which a tameable animal is tied, employing untamed quadrupeds, throwing sticks, mud, stones, rods, or arrows on chariots or elephants, raising or waiving the arm against chariots or elephants, shall also be treated as assault.p.333

P011(The charioteer) who cries out (to a passer-by) „get out‟ shall not be punished for collision (samghattane).p.333punishment

P012A man who is hurt to death by an elephant under provocation (caused by himself) shall supply not only a kumbha of liquor (less by a drona), garlands, and scents but also as much cloth as is necessary to wash the tusks; for death caused by an elephant is as meritorious as the sacred bath taken at the end of a horse-sacrifice. Hence this offer (of liquor, etc.), is known as „washing the legs.‟p.333dronakumbha

P013When an indifferent passer-by is killed by an elephant the driver shall be punished with the highest amercement.p.333amercementhighest-amercementpunishment

P014When the owner of a horned or tusked animal does not rescue a man from being destroyed by his animal, he shall be punished with the first amercement. If he heedlessly keeps quite from rescuing though entreated, he shall be punished with twice the first amercement.p.333twiceamercementfirst-amercementpunishment

P015When a person causes or allows horned or tusked animals to destroy each other, he shall not only pay a fine equal to the value of the destroyed animal or animals, but also make good the loss (to the sufferer).p.334fine

P016When a man rides over an animal which is left off in the name of gods, or over a bull, an ox, or over a female calf, he shall be fined 500 panas. He who drives away the above animals shall be punished with the highest amercement.p.334500 panasfine 500 panas500amercementfinehighest-amercementpunishment

P017When a person carries off such inferior quadrupeds as are productive of wool or milk, or are useful for loading or riding, he shall not only pay a fine equal to their value, but also restore them.p.334fine

P018The same punishment shall be imposed in the case of driving away inferior quadrupeds for purposes other than ceremonials performed in honour of gods or ancestors.p.334punishment

P019When an animal which has its nose-string cut off or which is not well tamed to yoke causes hurt; or when an animal, either coming furiously against a man or receding backwards with the cart to which it is tied, causes hurt or when an animal causes hurt in confusion brought about by the thronging of people and other animals; the owner of the animal shall not be punished;. but for hurt caused to men under circumstances other than the above, fines shall be imposed as laid down before, while the loss of any animal life due to such causes shall be made good. If the driver of a cart or carriage causing hurt is a minor, the master inside the cart or carriage shall be punished. In the absence of the master, any person who is seated inside, or the driver himself if he has attained his majority shall be punished. Carts or carriages occupied by a minor or with no person shall be taken possession of by the king.p.334kingfinemutilationpunishment

P020Whatever a man attempts to do to others by witch-craft shall be (practically) applied to the doer himself. Witch-craft merely to arouse love in an indifferent wife, in a maiden by her lover, or in a wife by her husband is no offence. But when it is injurious to others, the doer shall be punished with the middle most amercement.p.335amercementpunishment

P021When a man performs witch-craft to win the sister of his own father or mother, the wife of a maternal uncle or of a preceptor, his own daughter-in-law, daughter, or sister, he shall have his limb cut off and also put to death, ,while any woman who yields herself to such an offender shall also, receive similar punishment. Any woman who yields herself to a slave, a servant, or a hired labourer shall be similarly punished.p.335labourerslavedeathmutilationpunishment

P022A Kshatriya who commits adultery with an unguarded Bráhman woman shall be punished with the highest amercement; a Vaisya doing the same shall be deprived of the whole of his property; and a Súdra shall be burnt alive wound round in mats.p.335bráhmansúdrabrahmankshatriyasudravaisyaamercementburninghighest-amercementpunishment

P023Whoever commits adultery with the queen of the land shall be burnt alive in a vessel (kumbhílpákah.)p.335kumbhílpákahqueenburning

P024A man who commits adultery with a woman of low caste shall be banished with prescribed mark branded on his fore-head, or shall be degraded to the same caste.p.335banishment

P025A Súdra or a svapáka who commits adultery with a woman of low caste shall be put to death, while the woman shall have her ears and nose cut off.p.335svapákasúdrasudradeathmutilation

P026Adultery with a nun (pravrajitá) shall be punishable with a fine of 24 panas while the nun who submits herself shall also pay a similar fine.p.33524 panasfine 24 panas24pravrajitáfinepunishment

P027A man who forces his connection with a harlot shall be fined 12 panas.p.33612 panasfine 12 panas12fine

P028When many persons perform witch-craft towards a single woman, each of them shall be punished with a fine of 24 panas.p.33624 panasfine 24 panas24finepunishment

P029When a man has connection with a woman against the order of nature (a-yonau), he shall be punished with the first amercement.p.336amercementfirst-amercementpunishment

P030A man having sexual intercourse with another man shall also pay the first amercement.p.336amercementfirst-amercement

P031* When a senseless man has sexual intercourse with beasts, he shall be fined 12 panas; when he comits the same act with idols (representatives) of goddesses (daivatapratimá), he shall be fined twice as much.p.33612 panasfine 12 panas12twicedaivatapratimáfine

P032* When the king punishes an innocent man, he shall throw into water dedicating to god Varuna a fine equal to thirty times the unjust imposition; and this amount shall afterwards be distributed among the Bráhmans.p.336thirtybráhmansbrahmankingfine

P033* By this act, the king will be free from the sin of unjust imposition; for king Varuna is the ruler of sinners among men.p.336king
