  • B02.C26.T1.P003 Book II · The Superintendent of Slaughter-HouseWhen a person entraps, kills, or molests either fish or birds that do not prey upon other animals, he shall be fined 26¾ panas; and when he does the same to deer and other beasts, he shall be fined twice as much.
