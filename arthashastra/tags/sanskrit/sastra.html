sástra

Sanskrit terms · appears 5 times

  • B01.C01.T17.P003 Book I · The Life of a KingThis Sástra, bereft of undue enlargement and easy to grasp and understand, has been composed by Kautilya in words the meaning of which has been definitely settled.
  • B03.C01.T8.P012 Book III · Determination of Forms of Agreement; Determination of Legal Disputes* But whenever sacred law (sástra) is conflict with rational law (Dharmanyáya=kings' law), then reason shall be held authoritative; for there the original text (on which the sacred law has been based) is not available.
  • B15.C01.T1.P035 Book XV · Paragraphical Divisions of This Treatise* Thus this Sástra, conforming to these paragraphic divisions is composed as a guide to acquire and secure this and the other world.
  • B15.C01.T1.P036 Book XV · Paragraphical Divisions of This Treatise* In the light of this Sástra one cannot only set on foot righteous, economical, and aesthetical acts and maintain them, but also put down unrighteous, uneconomical and displeasing acts.
  • B15.C01.T1.P037 Book XV · Paragraphical Divisions of This Treatise* This Sástra has been made by him who from intolerance (of misrule) quickly rescued the scriptures and the science of weapons and the earth which had passed to the Nanda king.
