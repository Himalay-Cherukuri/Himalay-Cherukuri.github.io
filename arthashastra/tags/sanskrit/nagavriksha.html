nágavriksha

Sanskrit terms · appears 2 times

  • B02.C11.T1.P070 Book II · Examination of Gems that are to be Entered into the TreasuryNágavriksha (a species of a tree), Likucha (Artocarpus Lakucha), and Vakula (Mimusops Elengi), and Vata (Ficus Indica) are the sources (of their fibres).
  • B02.C11.T1.P071 Book II · Examination of Gems that are to be Entered into the TreasuryThat of Nágavriksha is yellow (pita); that of Likucha is of the colour of wheat; that of Vakula is white; and the rest is of the colour of butter.
