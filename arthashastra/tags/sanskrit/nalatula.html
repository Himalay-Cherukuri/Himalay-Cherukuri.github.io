nalatúla

Sanskrit terms · appears 2 times

  • B02.C11.T1.P057 Book II · Examination of Gems that are to be Entered into the TreasurySátina, Nalatúla, and Vrittapuchchha are the skins of aquatic animals (Audra).
  • B02.C11.T1.P058 Book II · Examination of Gems that are to be Entered into the TreasurySátina is black; Nalatúla is of the colour of the fibre of Nala, a kind of grass; and Vrittapuchchha (that which possesses a round tail) is brown.
