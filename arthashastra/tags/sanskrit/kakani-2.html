kákaní

Sanskrit terms · appears 3 times

  • B02.C13.T1.P032 Book II · Superintendent of Gold in the Goldsmith's OfficeThe colouring ingredient of gold is one kákaní of tíkshna which is of the colour of the neck of a peacock, tinged with white, and which is dazzling and full of copper (pitapúrnitam).
  • B02.C19.T1.P025 Book II · The Superintendent of Weights and MeasuresTraders shall every day pay one kákaní to the Superintendent towards the charge of stamping the weights and measures.
  • B04.C10.T1.P008 Book IV · Fines in Lieu of Mutilation of LimbsWhen a gambler substitutes false dice to be hired for a kákaní or any other accessory things of dice-play, or commits fraud by tricks of hand, he shall have his hand cut off or pay a fine of 400 panas.
