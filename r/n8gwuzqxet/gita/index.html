Śrīmad Bhagavad Gītā — Sādhaka-Sañjīvanī

Commentary by Swami Ramsukhdas

Sādhaka-Sañjīvanī (“the life-giving herb for the seeker”) is Swami Ramsukhdas’s verse-by-verse commentary on the Śrīmad Bhagavad Gītā. Each chapter page gives, for every verse: the transliterated Sanskrit, the English translation, and the commentary (including the later Appendix notes), followed by the chapter’s colophon.
